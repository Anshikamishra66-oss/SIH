Problem Statement

Government APMC Mandis are the lifeline for millions of Indian farmers seeking Minimum Support Price (MSP) compensation. However, the current physical procurement system is severely plagued by the following issues:

🚜

Mandi Congestion

Thousands of tractor-trolleys arriving simultaneously, causing kilometers-long traffic bottlenecks and severe chaos.

⏳

Exhausting Waiting

Farmers forced to camp at mandi yards for 24–72 hours awaiting their turn with no visibility into queue status.

🌧️

Grain Spoilage

Open grain bags exposed to sudden rains, moisture, pests, and pilferage during prolonged waiting periods.

👁️

No Queue Visibility

Zero visibility into queue status leads to farmer anxiety, gate-rushing, and frequent disputes at mandis.

💳

Payment Opacity

Uncertainty about DBT timelines, UTR issuance, and bank credit causes financial stress for farmers.

📋

Officer Overload

Mandi officers overwhelmed by manual token registers, weighing slips, and paper-based tally sheets.

💡

Core Solution & Innovations

01

📅 Guaranteed Time-Slot Reservation

Farmers select their nearest APMC Mandi and book a convenient 1-hour time window. Mandi intake is hard-capped per slot, eliminating overbooking. Generates a digital Token (TK-1001) with QR code & SMS confirmation.

02

⏱️ Real-Time Queue & Turn Tracker

Farmers monitor the live token being served on their mobile phone. Displays exact number of farmers ahead and an AI-calibrated Estimated Turn Time so farmers arrive 15 minutes before their turn.

03

🏢 Mandi Officer Operations Console

Counter-specific announcer panel with one-click "Call Next Token". Digital gate check-in and produce grading modal records weight, quality grade (A/B/C), moisture %, and calculates official MSP payout.

04

💳 DBT Payout Pipeline & Analytics

Automatic payment initialization on procurement completion. 8-stage visual progress timeline from slot reservation to bank credit. State & District Command Dashboard with live tonnage analytics and CSV exports.

Event Name Direction Payload Description

Security Architecture

🔑 JWT Authentication

  • Stateless tokens signed with secret key
  • Automatic 7-day expiration policy
  • Axios interceptor for automatic header injection
  • 401 auto-refresh and re-login redirect

🔒 Password Security

  • Bcrypt.js with 12 salt rounds
  • Pre-save Mongoose middleware hashing
  • toJSON suppression (never exposed in API)
  • Express Validator sanitizes all inputs

🛡️ Middleware Security

  • Helmet.js sets secure HTTP headers
  • CORS policy restricts allowed origins
  • Rate limiting on auth endpoints
  • Role middleware enforces RBAC on every route

⚛️ Atomic Slot Locking

  • MongoDB findOneAndUpdate with $inc operator
  • Prevents race condition on simultaneous bookings
  • Duplicate booking check before slot reservation
  • Automatic status transition: available → full

🌾 Impact & Vision

Kisan Procurement Connect is built to empower Indian agriculture with transparent, corruption-free, and dignified procurement for every farmer across the nation. By replacing chaotic manual processes with a fully digital, real-time platform, it eliminates the 24–72 hour waiting crisis, prevents grain spoilage, ensures guaranteed MSP payments via Direct Benefit Transfer, and provides measurable accountability at every stage — from slot booking to bank account credit. This is the future of agricultural procurement in India.
